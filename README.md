**What is the Seu Zé de Madureira trend?**
It is a viral Brazilian video trend where a character dances the classic Baile Charme passinho style. With Charme Dance AI you can put yourself, a friend or any photo into that same dance move.

**Do I need any photos besides one picture?**
No. One clear photo of a person is enough — the AI animates the rest.
